# InaiSec landing-page content — v1

## The job of this page

Start a useful first conversation with a security or incident-response leader at a company that handles customer data. The page should sell a founder-led working conversation, not an unfinished product or a generic product tour.

**Voice:** clear-eyed, specific, human, and outcome-led.  
**Promise:** better decision readiness after a serious incident—not automated legal conclusions, broad integrations, or a replacement for the existing stack.

## Narrative spine

An alert can be confirmed while the business answer is still unclear. The evidence fragments across systems and teams; the decision gets harder to defend. InaiSec is being shaped with practitioners around a better way to establish shared facts, ownership, and a decision record. The first step is a practical conversation about one hard incident.

## 2. Problem

**Eyebrow**  
The problem

**Headline**  
One incident. Four versions of the truth.

**Body**  
Detection can confirm that an alert is real. It rarely answers what the business needs to decide next. Analysts still pull evidence from identity, cloud, SaaS, tickets, and notes; leadership, counsel, and customer teams enter the conversation with only part of the story.

By the time those views meet, the next call often has to be made before the picture is complete.

**Four tension cards**

| Gap | Question in the room |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence | What do we know, and what is still missing or disputed? |
| Impact | What could this mean for systems, customers, and the business? |
| Decision | Who owns the next call, and what evidence is enough to make it? |
| Record | Can we explain why we made that call later? |

**Why this comes from the original page**  
It restores the concrete, credible incident reality: manual evidence stitching and cross-system coordination. It should not use the old unsupported market statistics.

## 7. FAQ

Keep this as a short accordion after the conversation CTA. It should clear objections, not restart the sales pitch.

**Is this another AI SOC or detection tool?**  
No. Detection matters, but this conversation starts once a signal is confirmed and people need to align on facts, impact, ownership, and the next decision.

**Do we have to replace our existing stack?**  
No. InaiSec is not a replacement for detection, containment, counsel, or human approval.

**Do you need our data or production credentials?**  
Not for a first conversation or tabletop. A spoken, non-identifying incident story or realistic scenario is enough; we do not ask for a data upload or production access.

**Is this legal advice?**  
No. The focus is a clearer decision record and better cross-functional coordination. Legal conclusions remain with your counsel.

**Who is this most useful for?**  
Security and incident-response leaders at customer-data companies who have lived an incident where scope, impact, ownership, or customer communication was hard to defend.

**What does a design partner do?**  
If there is a fit after the first conversation, we may invite you to a deeper working session around a realistic tabletop. The purpose is to shape the workflow and decide together whether a paid design partnership makes sense.
